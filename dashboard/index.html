How the ranking works

Eligibility: a protocol records no token at all. The board ranks all 40 watchlist names plus every pre-token protocol outside the watchlist that passes the eligibility test — 157 of them in this snapshot, 197 rows in all. The watchlist is the floor, not the ceiling: twenty above-floor protocols pass the same test and rank at the same curve value, no discount — every snapshot-testable reason the house watchlist might omit them (category, version, name, exchange-name beginning, parent-name linkage, duplication) was tested and fails, and no figure in the snapshot sets a haircut for them. Excluded are wrapped, staked or bridged products of a token that already trades: the Bridge, Canonical Bridge, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking, Restaked BTC and CEX categories, versioned duplicate pools (a name ending in V2/v2/2.0), and any name carrying restak, stak or wrapped — none of those is a standalone launch. Protocols whose token already trades are set aside, never ranked; the "Already trading" tab shows the top 24 of them at their market cap, so the board never mixes a model's claim with a market price.

Source: the sealed DefiLlama snapshot of 14 November 2026 (758 protocols). Implied value is this entry's own claim from those figures — it does not predict whether or when any token is issued.
